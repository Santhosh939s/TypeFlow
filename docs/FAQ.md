# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How is WPM calculated in TypeFlow?
TypeFlow follows standard international typist conventions:
$$\text{WPM} = \frac{\text{Correct Characters} / 5}{\text{Minutes}}$$
Spaces submitted between words count as characters.

### What is the difference between Raw WPM and Net WPM?
* **Raw WPM**: Total characters typed (including mistakes) divided by 5 per minute.
* **Net WPM**: WPM penalized by uncorrected typing errors, reflecting true clean speed.

### Do I need an account to practice?
No. TypeFlow is fully functional offline and in guest mode. Scores are preserved locally in browser storage. An account is only required to synchronize scores across devices and appear on global leaderboards.

### How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
Open an issue on the [TypeFlow GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Santhosh939s/TypeFlow/issues).
